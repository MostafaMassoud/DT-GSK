Date: 2026-07-31. Addendum 1 signing commit `5c9bfae82`; Amendment 1 recorded

## A3.1 The factual error and its authority

Three pre-registered passages assert the win: the Section 3 directional
expectation ("AGSK won the CEC2020 competition; Mohamed et al. 2020 is a
co-author's paper"), the Section 9 [AGSK first] outcome variant ("the CEC2020
competition it won"), and the Section 10 conflict-adjacency disclosure ("AGSK
won the CEC2020 competition and Mohamed et al. (2020) is a co-author's paper");
Amendment 2 §A2.3 echoes the same clause.

The corpus's own sanctioned source — `apgsk2021` (IEEE Access 9, p. 65936), a
co-author's paper, already cited in the manuscript — states: "Actually, AGSK
[19] is the runner up in CEC2020 competition", and its Section IV.C records
IMODE as the winner, AGSK as the runner-up, and J2020 third. The corpus's
evidence card for `mohamed2020agsk` explicitly warns against sourcing a
competition-win claim to the AGSK paper. No corpus source supports "won".

The error inflates the comparator's pedigree in the direction that most
flatters DT-GSK's registered fourth place, so under the addendum's own
standards it cannot stand.

## A3.2 Corrected wording (binding from this amendment forward)

The Section 9 [AGSK first] variant, with the ordinal slot filled by Amendment 2
and ONLY the false subordinate clause corrected, now reads — and every
manuscript locus that carries the bank sentence must carry it verbatim:

> On AGSK's strongest suite — the CEC2020 competition in which it was the
> runner-up — DT-GSK places **fourth**; the family panel corroborates AGSK's
> published strength in this regime, consistent with the tiering thesis: every
> dimension-gated DT-GSK subsystem is inactive at D <= 20.

Citation attachment: the manuscript attaches `\cite{apgsk2021}` immediately
after "runner-up" at every bank locus EXCEPT the abstract (which carries no
citations by journal style); the citation marker is outside the registered
wording and does not vary it.

The Section 10 conflict-adjacency disclosure correspondingly reads:

> AGSK was the runner-up of the CEC2020 competition (behind IMODE; apgsk2021,
> p. 65936) and Mohamed et al. (2020) is a co-author's paper. This adjacency
> is disclosed here, in the manuscript wherever CEC2020 results are
> interpreted, and in the conflicts block — stated by the authors, not
> discovered by reviewers.

Amendment 2 §A2.3's closing echo is corrected the same way: "AGSK was the
runner-up of this competition and its paper is a co-author's." The Section 3
directional expectation is read with the same correction; its substance — AGSK
and APGSK are favored on their home regime — is unchanged and, if anything,
understated by the correction, since the directional expectation never needed
the win claim.

## A3.3 What does not change

DT-GSK places fourth of seven (4.1125); AGSK first in the family panel
(2.0875); the five-of-twenty-four Holm split (three favourable, two
unfavourable) and the prohibition on superiority claims over AGSK or FDB-AGSK;
the tiering-thesis reading of the outcome; every analysis id, tier label, tie
rule, and number in Amendments 1-2. The correction strengthens rather than
weakens the boundary-finding interpretation: the panel's AGSK-first result
corroborates the published strength of the competition's runner-up on its home
regime.
