# Terminology sheet — DT-GSK manuscript

**Purpose (ticket E-008).** The manuscript uses ~92 terms that appear in both
hyphenated and open form. Most of that variation is correct English, not
inconsistency, but nothing recorded the rule, so a reviewer counting dual forms
could not tell a correct pair from a defect. This sheet states the rule and lists
the controlled terms.

## The rule

**Hyphenate a compound modifier before the noun it modifies; leave it open
elsewhere.** This is the standard attributive-hyphenation convention.

| Attributive (hyphenated) | Predicative / nominal (open) |
|---|---|
| a *dimension-tiered* scaffold | the design is *dimension tiered* |
| the *accepted-move* signal | learned from *accepted moves* |
| a *budget-fair* protocol | the protocol is *budget fair* |
| *run-level* statistics | reported at *run level* |
| a *per-function* mean | computed *per function* |
| the *upper-tier* controllers | active in the *upper tier* |

Both spellings of such a pair are therefore expected in the text, and finding
both is not by itself evidence of drift. What *would* be a defect is the same
term hyphenated in the same grammatical role in one place and open in another.

## Controlled terms (one spelling only, in every role)

These are names or fixed technical terms, not compound modifiers, so they do not
vary with position:

| Term | Notes |
|---|---|
| GSK family | the noun phrase; *GSK-family* only attributively ("the GSK-family panel") |
| DT-GSK | always hyphenated; never "DT GSK" or "DTGSK" |
| interaction-structure memory (ISM) | always hyphenated, including nominally |
| gaining-sharing knowledge | always hyphenated (the algorithm's own name) |
| win/tie/loss | slashes, never hyphens or spaces |
| Holm-corrected | attributive only; otherwise "corrected with Holm" |
| Vargha--Delaney | en-dash between two surnames, not a hyphen |
| fitness--distance-balance | en-dash between the two concepts, hyphen inside the compound |

## Scope of verification

The rule above was applied to the terms listed here. The wider dual-form list
extracted from the rendered PDF was **not** verified term by term; per the
ticket, that check is what this sheet makes possible rather than something it
records as done. Any future audit should test role-correctness against the rule,
not merely count dual forms.
