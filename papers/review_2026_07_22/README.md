# Internal review round — 2026-07-22

**These are the authors' own internal review artifacts. They are not reports
from journal referees, and no journal peer review is recorded anywhere in this
repository.**

## What this directory is

On 2026-07-22 the authors applied
[`papers/PAPER_REVIEW_PROMPT.md`](../PAPER_REVIEW_PROMPT.md) — the project's
internal quality-assurance instrument — to the then-current manuscript package.
The instrument asks a reviewer to adopt a hostile, verification-first posture
across several specialist "seats" (science, statistics, reproducibility,
editorial, ethics/compliance, and so on), and to report findings with exact
file and line references.

The files here are the output of that exercise: simulated review reports, an
issue register, a readiness assessment, and per-topic audits. Filenames such as
`independent_reviewer_reports.md`, `desk_screening_report.md` and
`ethics_and_compliance_audit.md` name the *role being simulated*, not an
external body.

The round was run with AI assistance. The manuscript's *Use of Generative
Artificial Intelligence* statement is the authoritative disclosure of AI use in
this project; the README section "Internal Quality-Assurance Instruments" gives
the fuller framing.

## Why the unflattering findings are still here

This directory contains confirmed findings against the authors' own work,
including a Critical ticket raised against the paper's AI-disclosure wording at
the time. **That is deliberate.** The point of running an adversarial review is
to surface problems; deleting the record afterwards would defeat it. Findings
were adjudicated by the authors, and their dispositions — including the ones
rejected on evidence — are recorded in the register alongside the originals.

Where a ticket here reports a defect, check its disposition and the later
governance record before assuming it is still open: several were resolved in
subsequent freeze passes, and the manuscript has since advanced through two
further review rounds (registers dated 2026-07-31 in
[`papers/governance/`](../governance/)).

## Provenance caveat

This round predates the project's move to a standalone public repository. Some
files reference the originating private monorepo, its paths, or its commit
identifiers. Those identifiers do not resolve in this repository's history —
see the "Repository history and provenance" note in the top-level
[`README.md`](../../README.md).
