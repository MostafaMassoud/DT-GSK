# Academic Research Guidelines

Writing-craft and style references supplied by the owner on 2026-08-07 for use
while drafting and revising the manuscript. Files were staged with UUID names
and renamed to the corpus convention on the same day; identification is from
each document's own front matter (years appear in a filename only where the
document prints one — nothing is inferred).

**Role and scope.** These are *authoring guidelines*, not research sources:

- they are **not** part of the required-papers registry
  (`reference_papers/_metadata/`), are never cited by the manuscript, and must
  not be added to the registry seed;
- they are internal working references only and are **excluded from any public
  artifact** (the APA Publication Manual in particular is a copyrighted book;
  the standing rule in the reproducibility plan — no third-party PDF
  redistribution — applies to this folder with no exceptions).

## How the manuscript stands against them (checked 2026-08-07)

- **CARS**: the Introduction already realises the three moves — Move 1
  (territory: the proliferation of human-inspired metaheuristics and its
  critique corpus), Move 2 (niche: unaudited ports, under-determined published
  descriptions, no provenance-audited comparison, and terminal-only
  evaluation), Move 3 (occupying: the two-level audited benchmark plus the
  budget-wide convergence stream, the enumerated contributions, and the paper
  map). No structural change required.
- **APA-7**: enforced mechanically by gate G-APA over the rendered PDFs and by
  the banked APA sidecar layer (amendment A1; corrected method layer A3).
- **IEEE math conventions**: the manuscript's macros set statistics
  (`\mathrm{MaxNFE}`, upright test names, italic variables) consistently with
  the guide; venue typography itself is the MDPI class's.
- **Phrasebank**: consulted during revision; no mechanical gate is appropriate
  for phraseology.
